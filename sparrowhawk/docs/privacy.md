# Privacy Policy — Sparrowhawk

*Last Updated: May 17, 2026*

## Our Commitment
Blackpiranha is committed to your privacy. We believe that an RSS reader should be a personal tool, not a data collection instrument.

## Data Collection
**Sparrowhawk does not collect or transmit any of your personal or sensitive user information to external servers.**

The app does not collect personal identifiers (such as name, email, or phone number), device identifiers, or location data. All core data is handled exclusively on your device:

* **Feed Subscriptions:** URLs of the RSS/Atom feeds you follow.
* **Reading History:** Articles read or bookmarked.
* **App Settings:** Theme preferences, layout options, and typography configurations.

## Data Processing
All data processing and article caching occurs **locally on your device** via a private Room database. Direct network requests are made solely to fetch content from the feed providers you subscribe to, with no intermediary tracking servers.

## Data Deletion
You retain complete control over your data:
* **In-App:** Delete individual feeds or wipe reading history inside the settings screen.
* **System Settings:** Clear storage at any time via **Android Settings > Apps > Sparrowhawk > Storage > Clear Data**.
* **Uninstallation:** Removing the application permanently deletes all local databases and cached records.

## Third-Party Services
Sparrowhawk does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or advertising frameworks.

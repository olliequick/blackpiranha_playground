# Privacy Policy — Sparrowhawk

*Last Updated: May 17, 2026*[cite: 2]

## Our Commitment
Blackpiranha is committed to your privacy[cite: 2]. We believe that an RSS reader should be a personal tool, not a data collection instrument[cite: 2].

## Data Collection
**Sparrowhawk does not collect or transmit any of your personal or sensitive user information to external servers.**[cite: 2]

The app does not collect personal identifiers (such as name, email, or phone number), device identifiers, or location data[cite: 2]. All core data is handled exclusively on your device[cite: 2]:

* **Feed Subscriptions:** URLs of the RSS/Atom feeds you follow[cite: 2].
* **Reading History:** Articles read or bookmarked[cite: 2].
* **App Settings:** Theme preferences, layout options, and typography configurations[cite: 2].

## Data Processing
All data processing and article caching occurs **locally on your device** via a private Room database[cite: 2]. Direct network requests are made solely to fetch content from the feed providers you subscribe to, with no intermediary tracking servers[cite: 2].

## Data Deletion
You retain complete control over your data[cite: 2]:
* **In-App:** Delete individual feeds or wipe reading history inside the settings screen[cite: 2].
* **System Settings:** Clear storage at any time via **Android Settings > Apps > Sparrowhawk > Storage > Clear Data**[cite: 2].
* **Uninstallation:** Removing the application permanently deletes all local databases and cached records[cite: 2].

## Third-Party Services
Sparrowhawk does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or advertising frameworks[cite: 2].

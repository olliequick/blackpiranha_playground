# Eat What Ah

A lightweight food companion app for Android to help you log favorite spots and decide what to eat.

---

## Features

* **Quick Logging:** Save favorite dishes, stalls, and restaurant spots.
* **Decision Assistant:** Randomizer and filtering to break meal indecision.
* **Local Storage:** Fast and private, keeping all your food notes directly on your device.

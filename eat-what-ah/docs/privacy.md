# Privacy Policy — Eat What Ah

*Last Updated: 2026*

## Overview
Eat What Ah operates locally on your device.

* **Data Collection:** No personal details, analytics, or behavioral tracking are collected.
* **Storage:** All saved entries remain stored on your local device.
* **Deletion:** Uninstalling the app or clearing application cache removes all data.

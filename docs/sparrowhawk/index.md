# Sparrowhawk

**Enabling your doomscrolling in comfort and style**[cite: 1]

A modern RSS reader for Android[cite: 1]. Built for readers who value simplicity, clean typography, and dedicated buttons to navigate between articles[cite: 1, 19].

---

## Why Sparrowhawk?

* **📖 Modern Reading Interface**[cite: 1]  
  Developed with a clean Material 3 interface[cite: 1]. Features dedicated navigation buttons between articles without needing to constantly flick or swipe across the screen[cite: 1, 19].

* **🛠️ Powerful Management**[cite: 1]  
  Automated feed discovery—just paste a website URL[cite: 1]. Organize your sources with custom categories and full OPML import/export support[cite: 1, 3].

* **🎨 Deep Customization**[cite: 1]  
  Material 3 design with dynamic colors, Light and Dark themes, and support for Google Fonts to personalize your reading typography[cite: 1, 3, 19].

* **🧹 Privacy First**[cite: 1]  
  All data stays on your device[cite: 1]. Local Room database storage and foreground-only maintenance preserve your battery and privacy with no logins required[cite: 1, 2, 3].

* **✨ Free!**[cite: 1]  
  Use Sparrowhawk without limitations for free[cite: 1].

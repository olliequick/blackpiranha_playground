# Privacy Policy — WhereBus

*Last Updated: May 17, 2026*

## Our Commitment
Blackpiranha is committed to your privacy. WhereBus is designed to be a local dashboard utility without intrusive tracking or data collection.

## Data Collection
**WhereBus does not collect or transmit any of your personal or sensitive user information to external servers.**

The app does not collect personal identifiers, device identifiers, or location data. Bus schedule network requests are made directly to official transit data providers or APIs as configured by your station selection, with no intermediary tracking servers.

## Data Processing
All configuration and station preferences are stored **locally on your device**.

## Data Deletion
You retain complete control over your data:
* **System Settings:** Clear storage at any time via **Android Settings > Apps > WhereBus > Storage > Clear Data**.
* **Uninstallation:** Removing the application permanently deletes all local records and settings.

## Third-Party Services
WhereBus does not include any third-party analytics SDKs, trackers, or advertising frameworks.

## Contact
Questions regarding this policy can be directed to: `wherebus.app (at) blackpiranha.net`

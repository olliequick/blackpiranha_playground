# WhereBus

**Dashboard for bus arrival times at your specific bus station**

WhereBus is an Android application designed as a large-display dashboard for bus arrival times at a specific bus station. Rather than replacing full-featured transit apps, WhereBus is built specifically for individuals or families who want a very large display on their mobile devices showing when the next bus is going to arrive.

---

## Why WhereBus?

* **🚌 Large Display Dashboard**  
  Optimized for tablets and large form-factor devices. Display it in a common area in the house to keep everyone informed at a glance.

* **⏱️ Precise Departure Timing**  
  Make a move to leave the house only at the precise time to arrive at the bus stop when your desired bus arrives.

* **🧹 Privacy First**  
  All configuration and bus station data stay locally on your device with no unnecessary tracking or accounts required.

* **✨ Free!**  
  Use WhereBus without limitations for free.
